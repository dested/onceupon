# App name and domain

- **Date:** 2026-09-22
- **Status:** done (Sal locked Squiggletale, 2026-09-22; recorded in decisions.md)
- **Type:** analysis
- **What:** why "Once Upon" cannot ship, the App Store landscape, candidate names with conflict and domain checks, and a recommendation. Sal: "it doesn't really have the drawing or storybook aspect or the fact that it's custom... this thing has to explode." .app is fine (Sal, same day).

## Once Upon is out

- "Once Upon" is a registered mark (Intl. Reg. 1603797, class 42) held by the Swedish photo-book company behind onceupon.photo, a parent-facing brand in an adjacent category.
- "Once Upon – A Storybook App" already ships personalized illustrated kids' stories on the App Store, so the brand search would be contested forever.
- Thousands of "Once Upon a ..." apps and books dilute it; it says "story" and nothing about drawing, voice, or the child being the author.

"Once Upon a Crayon" (both domains free) fixes the drawing angle but inherits the mark and the storybook-app collision. Fallback only.

## What the name has to do

1. A four-year-old can say it and a parent can spell it after hearing it once (the share loop is word of mouth: a kid on video, a grandparent forwarding a link, "Made with X" on the end card).
2. Ownable: a coined word or an uncommon compound with a clean USPTO path. The sweep shows the durable kids brands are coined (Toca Boca, Sago Mini, Pok Pok, Lingokids, StoryBots); descriptive keyword strings ("AI Story Maker for Kids", "My Story", "Kids Doodle") sit at the bottom and are interchangeable.
3. Signals story and drawing. Voice and "their own words" go in the subtitle; no one-word name carries all three.
4. `crayon` is a near-uncontested App Store term (barely appears in competitor titles) while `story`, `stories`, `drawing`, `doodle`, `bedtime`, `kids` are the head terms. Brand in the name, head terms in the 30-char subtitle and the 100-char keyword field.
5. Domain free on .app (and ideally .com), handles free.

## Landscape (App Store, kids story and drawing)

Three camps. AI story generators (type a prompt, get a narrated book): Scribblo, StoryCraft, StoryTimeBuddy, AiMagic, Storytime AI, Oscar Stories, DreamTales, Bedtimestory.ai, Imagine Tales, Storybird, Dreamland (has a "Draw to Story" mode), DoodleTale, TaleO, Scribli, Scribbly Books. Kid-authored draw-and-tell: Draw and Tell (Duck Duck Moose / Khan), My Story, Crayola Create and Play, Kids Doodle, Doodle Buddy, Magic Crayon Painting, PicaBook, Story Doodle. Big low-stim brands: Toca Boca, Sago Mini, Pok Pok, Lingokids, Epic, Khan Kids, StoryBots. Our app straddles the first two camps, the most crowded naming lane; nobody in it has "the kid talks and it draws live".

## Recommendation

**Squiggletale**, with `squiggletale.com` and `squiggletale.app`.

- It is the product in one word: a squiggle becomes a tale. Drawing and storybook are both in it, which is exactly what Sal said "Once Upon" lacked.
- Coined, so it is ownable and the brand search is ours from day one; it sits in the Toca Boca / Pok Pok / Sago Mini register that Apple's Kids editors feature.
- A kid can shout it on a TikTok and a grandparent can spell it. "I made a Squiggletale" is a sentence.
- Marketing writes itself around squiggle / giggle / wiggle; the mascot is a crayon squiggle with eyes.
- Voice and "their own words" live in the subtitle and the end card, where they belong.

Listing: name `Squiggletale`, subtitle `Say a story, the crayon draws` (29), keywords `story,stories,bedtime,kids,drawing,doodle,crayon,tell,talk,imagination,toddler,preschool,book`, end card `Made with Squiggletale · squiggletale.app`.

Second choice, if the name should be plainer for grandparents: **Crayon Tales** (`crayontales.app`). Third, if the voice mechanic must be in the name: **Speakadoodle**. Do not ship Story Crayon as the name; use it as subtitle material.

## Buy-day checklist for the pick

1. Register .com and .app (and .co if cheap) at once; the RDAP check is a snapshot.
2. Grab @name on Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X; check the Apple developer team name.
3. USPTO TESS search, then file an intent-to-use application in classes 9 (downloadable software) and 41 (entertainment / education); about $350 a class.
4. Create the app record in App Store Connect to reserve the name (30 chars, must be unique across the store).
5. Rename in the repo and docs: `drawLogo()` placeholder wordmark in `src/export/frame.ts`, the end card, `index.html` title, the go-to-market plan's listing copy and Apple Ads brand terms.
