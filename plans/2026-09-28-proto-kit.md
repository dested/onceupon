# Proto: kit dialect

- Date: 2026-09-28
- Status: active
- Type: plan
- What: `kit` dialect (src/proto/kit/): the model says WHAT, an offline-authored crayon library draws HOW

## Idea

In ops, the model draws every shape. That costs about 340 output tokens a beat, around 3.2 s to finish, and it breaks when the model self-corrects. In kit, the model writes one line per subject, and a library of about 140 hand-authored kinds plus 21 place presets renders the finished art. The ops grammar stays as a freehand fallback for anything not in the catalog.

## Grammar

- `place <preset> [day|sunset|night] [rain|snow] ["title"] [keep=a,b]`: paints a full backdrop (sky, back, land layers) and doubles as the page turn.
- `<kind> [id] x [y] [colors…] [mood] [left] [big|small|tiny|huge|s=N] [sit|sleep|fly|swim] [wear=a,b] [eyes=N] [in=|on=|at=id] ["name"]`:
  - draws a subject;
  - re-issuing an existing id updates it (a new x walks it there; a new mood, colors or wear redraws it in place with a single reset).
- `hide <id> <behind>`: hops the subject behind scenery (layer below the target); the next move brings it back.
- Everything else goes to the ops parser: move, pose, say, fx, rm, recolor, ent/draw/face.
- The CURRENT PAGE snapshot is one terse line per kit subject: kind, id, x, y, colors, mood, pose, facing, scale, wear, in, top y, mouth anchor.

## Library

Files are in `library/`:
- people: 27 roles
- animals: 22 side-view species
- critters: 25 front, bird, water and bug kinds
- creatures: 9 fantasy kinds
- objects: vehicles, scenery and things
- wear: 20 accessories
- places: 21 presets

Faces use dot eyes with a catchlight and engine `faceShapes` mouths. Kinds have color slots, moods and poses. Containers (boat, bed, car, nest…) have seat and front layers. Props have surface and mouth anchors.

## Known weaknesses / next

- Rider placement:
  - `pig in=car` renders the pig standing on the car roof;
  - wear on a vehicle is accepted, so the car got sunglasses.
- A `fly` pose persists after gliding to the ground, so the dragon looks like it is lying down. Landing should reset the pose.
- There is no overlap avoidance: an alien over a dragon, a wave over a dinosaur.
- Front critters sleep upright. The horse's lying pose is odd. The lion's ears are lost in its mane.
- The kite has no string to the holder, and there are no hand-held props yet (the `hand` anchor exists but isn't used).
- The Stage recall bug: `objectCreated` ignores obj.flipped, scale and anim. The dialect works around it by re-emitting sc/flip/anim; the real fix belongs in stage.ts.
- Faces are built by the kit (`_eye{i}`, `_eye{i}_pupil`, `_mouth` ids), so renderer blink/cheek logic keyed to face.ts must match.
- The live reveal speed of full-bleed backdrops is untested.
