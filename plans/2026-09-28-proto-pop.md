# Pop renderer style

- **Date:** 2026-09-28
- **Status:** active
- **Type:** plan
- **What:** a Stage look (`style: 'pop'`) that makes any dialect's shapes read like a picture book, at zero tokens and zero latency

## Why

The model's shapes are fine; the page was not. Fills were 62%-alpha hachure, so the sky showed through
every character and parts showed through each other; outlines were flat brown on everything; nothing
had light, faces were googly eyes, characters floated on blank paper. All of that is renderer work
that lifts every dialect (ops, kit, sketch) the same way.

## What pop does (engine/pop.ts, behind `new Stage(canvas, { style: 'pop' })`)

| Feature | How |
| --- | --- |
| Opaque fills | Every filled shape carries a paper knockout painted in the band of scan lines revealed so far: `destination-over` over the whole shape (hides objects behind) and source-over inside a ~1 unit inset (hides this object's earlier parts, never its own edge). Inset clipped to the shape so sharp tips cannot spike. |
| Waxy color | Denser main hachure (spacing 1.15, width x1.12, alpha 0.8) plus a lighter cross pass at another angle (alpha 0.32). |
| Light | Shading crescent: hachure over the shape minus itself nudged up-left (interval subtraction per scan line, plus an even-odd clip), in a same-hue shade. Highlight tick on round shapes, upper-left. |
| Line quality | An outline that inks a fill in generic brown/black/neutral gray is tinted toward `deepen(fill)`; white fills get a warm gray. Harder-edged wax dab sprite, lift at both ends, heavier on the underside. A fill with no explicit outline gets an edge in its own deeper ink. |
| Faces | `Shape.role` (additive, set by face.ts: eyeWhite/eye/pupil/mouth). Catchlight in each pupil, rosy cheeks, brows for sad/surprised (read from the mouth curve), eye rings never re-tinted. Seeded idle blink (period 3.2-5.8 s, 150 ms, lid in the color of the fill under the eye) through the Stage clock, live only (not in instant/snapshot mode). |
| Grounding | Soft radial contact shadow under anything whose bottom is within the ground line (-2.5..+7), fading in with the reveal. |
| Sky and ground | bg sky: vertical gradient wash (deeper at the top, lighter at the horizon) painted along the sweep, paper tooth punched out of it, near-horizontal texture strokes a shade darker. Page-wide filled rects are bands: same wash, only a soft top horizon line, and seeded texture by hue: fanned grass tufts, water ripples, sand specks. Dark sky: 28 seeded twinkling stars once the sky is drawn. |
| Paper | Same base color (knockouts paint it), deeper warm vignette, seeded fibers. |
| Life | Squash and stretch from the feet on the breath (1.1%). |

## Guarantees

- **Classic unchanged:** 31 classic jpgs of `pop-base` hashed before any engine edit, re-rendered after every change: identical bytes. Every pop branch is gated on `stroke.pop` / `style === 'pop'`; classic dab math is kept literally.
- **Deterministic:** all pop randomness is the shape's seeded rng or `hashString`; blink/stars/breath read only the Stage clock. Two pop renders: identical bytes.
- **Reveal:** region paints follow the hachure sweep of the stroke being revealed (band = offset of the crayon head ± 1.3 spacing), so nothing appears ahead of the crayon (checked in live snapshots, `scripts/proto-live.mjs`).
- **Cost:** per-frame render (headless Chrome, 1280x800): story-bunny classic p50 0.5 / p95 3.4 ms vs pop 0.6 / 2.9; dragon-fire-castle 16.0 / 20.6 vs 16.1 / 20.9 (that scene is heavy in both). Pop adds strokes (cross, shade, highlight) at layer paint time, not per frame; per-frame extras are one radial gradient per grounded object, 28 star dots at night, and blink circles.

## Tools

- `node scripts/proto-render.mjs <run> <dialect> style=pop port=<p>` renders `-bN.pop.jpg` beside the classic ones; `bun scripts/proto-sheet.ts <run>` shows both columns.
- `node scripts/proto-live.mjs <run> <dialect> <story,...> style=pop port=<p>` plays in real time, prints render p50/p95/max, writes `live-<story>.<style>-NN.jpg` every 500 ms.

## Tried and dropped

- Diagonal sky texture in the fill color: light gaps over a darker wash read as rain. Now near-horizontal and a shade darker.
- Short "W" grass tufts: read as paw prints, then as barcode combs. Now 2-4 fanned, tapered blades, denser at the horizon.
- Glossy highlight at 0.75 alpha, 62% white: plastic. Now 50% alpha.
- Outline 1.12x: too fat on the models' already bold shapes. Back to 1x with a harder dab.

## Not done / next

- Not wired into the studio: `session.ts` / replay / export construct `Stage` without `style`. Adding a setting is one line per call site; export determinism in pop is expected (same rules) but unverified on the mp4 path.
- Part-level motion (tail wag, walk cycle) needs per-part layers; out of scope.
- Indoor floors get sand specks (tan reads as sand); a wood-grain variant would need to know "indoors".
