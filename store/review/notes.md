Hello App Review team, we hope you're well.

Squiggletale is a storybook app for children aged 6 to 8: a child tells a story out loud, and a crayon draws it page by page. The first story is free (5 minutes), and there is a free 3-minute story every Saturday. A grown-up can buy more story minutes as consumable in-app purchases.
No account is needed.

Purchases:
- Tap the "..." button at the top right, then "Grown-ups". Answer the grown-up question (a multiplication), then tap "Buy minutes". The paywall shows the three minute packs.
- The paywall also opens on its own when the minutes run out. Its buy buttons ask the grown-up question first.
- App Review purchases use Apple's sandbox environment.
- "Restore purchases" is on the paywall and in Grown-ups.

Main review flow:
1. Open the app. A short picture-book intro plays. Tap "Next" to page through it, or the X at the top right to skip to the storybook.
2. Tap the big yellow microphone button.
3. Answer the grown-up question, then read the "Before the first story" card and tap "I agree".
4. Allow the Microphone and Speech Recognition prompts.
5. Say a story, for example: "A purple dragon named Pickles flew over a castle on a hill." The crayon draws it while the words show at the bottom.
6. Keep talking to add to the picture or turn the page. Say "The End" to finish.
7. The closing card says "Look what you imagined!". Tap "Play it again" to watch the story replay with the child's voice.
8. Tap the book button at the top left to see the bookshelf ("My stories").
9. Typing works too: tap the keyboard button next to the microphone and type a sentence.

Permissions and data:
- Microphone and Speech Recognition are requested only when the child first taps the microphone, after a grown-up has agreed on the "Before the first story" card.
- Speech is turned into words on the device by Apple's on-device speech recognition. The voice recording stays on the device, with the story, so it can play back.
- Only the text of the story's words is sent to our server, which asks a third-party AI service to turn them into a drawing. That service does not train on the words and deletes them within 30 days. We do not keep the words on our server.
- No ads, no third-party analytics, no tracking, no chat, and no content from other users.

Account and safety controls:
- Grown-ups (behind the grown-up question) contains Buy minutes, Restore purchases, the "Drawing with AI" switch (turns drawing off), Privacy, Terms, Support and Delete my data.
- Links out of the app, purchases and sharing ("Send to grandma" makes a private web link to the finished story) all ask the grown-up question first.
- The shared story page does not show the child's name.
