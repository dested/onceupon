# Marketing stories

Three stories, one per custom product page theme, told the way a 6–8 year old talks. Every preview, hero cut and theme page uses these exact words; what is on screen is what the child says.

Voice: ElevenLabs **Teddy Twinkle** (`XjGYkUkzth8BPs29fmcV`, young, American), model `eleven_v4`, rendered per line with timestamps by `scripts/marketing/voice.ts`. Files: `store/marketing/voice/<theme>-<n>.mp3`, word timings in `store/marketing/voice/<theme>.json`. Source of the text: `scripts/marketing/stories.ts`.

Natural kid speech: a 15-word sentence and a pause mid-sentence ("The owl said goodnight … and went to sleep") are on purpose; they exercise the relay fixes in 96921b8.

## Dragons and castles

1. Once upon a time a purple dragon lived in a big castle on a hill.
2. One day he flew up into the sky and found a rainbow.
3. The End!

## Bedtime

1. Once upon a time a sleepy owl lived in a tree.
2. At night the moon came out and the stars were twinkling.
3. The owl said goodnight and went to sleep.
4. The End!

## Ocean

1. Once upon a time a little green turtle lived in the sea.
2. She swam with a happy pink fish and they found a treasure chest.
3. The End!
